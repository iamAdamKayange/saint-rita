Policy

This policy explains how the Novena of Saint Rita of Cascia app stores and uses information, including the third-party services that help it work.

1. Taarifa tunazotumia

Programu haihitaji kufungua akaunti. Sala, maendeleo ya novena, historia, vipendwa, lugha, mandhari na muda wa vikumbusho huhifadhiwa kwenye kifaa chako kupitia hifadhi ya ndani ya programu.

Programu huomba ruhusa ya intaneti ili kupokea ujumbe wa Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) na kupakia matangazo. Huomba ruhusa ya arifa ili vikumbusho na ujumbe viweze kuonekana kwenye kifaa.

Hatukusanyi moja kwa moja jina, namba ya simu, orodha ya mawasiliano, eneo, picha au rekodi za sauti kupitia vipengele vya sasa vya programu. Ukituandikia kwa hiari, tutatumia taarifa utakazotuma kujibu ujumbe wako.

1. Information we use

The app does not require an account. Prayer content, novena progress, history, favorites, language, appearance, and reminder times are stored locally on your device using the app’s local storage.

The app requests internet access to receive Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) messages and load ads. It requests notification permission so reminders and messages can appear on your device.

The current app features do not directly collect your name, phone contacts, precise location, photos, or audio recordings. If you contact us voluntarily, we use the information you send to respond.

2. Huduma za nje

Firebase Cloud Messaging

FCM husaidia kutuma arifa za mbali. Firebase inaweza kuchakata tokeni ya kifaa/programu na taarifa za kiufundi zinazohitajika kupeleka ujumbe. Programu hujiunga na mada ya matangazo ya jumla rita_prayer_updates. Mipangilio ya vikumbusho vya ndani haiondoi yenyewe usajili huo wa mada. Unaweza kuzuia arifa zisionekane kupitia mipangilio ya arifa ya kifaa chako.

Google AdMob

Programu huonyesha matangazo kupitia AdMob. Google na washirika wake wanaweza kuchakata vitambulishi vya kifaa au vya matangazo, anwani ya IP, taarifa za programu/kifaa, mwingiliano na matangazo, na taarifa za uchunguzi ili kutoa, kupima na kuzuia matumizi mabaya ya matangazo. Mipangilio ya kifaa au Google inaweza kutoa njia za kudhibiti matangazo yaliyobinafsishwa kulingana na eneo lako.

Programu ya sasa haitumii Firebase Analytics au Crashlytics. Huduma hizi za nje zinatawaliwa pia na sera zao za faragha.

2. Third-party services

Firebase Cloud Messaging

FCM delivers remote notifications. Firebase may process an app/device token and technical information needed to deliver messages. The app subscribes to the general updates topic rita_prayer_updates. The in-app local reminder switches do not themselves remove that topic subscription. You can prevent notifications from appearing through your device’s notification settings.

Google AdMob

The app displays ads through AdMob. Google and its partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app/device information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse. Device or Google settings may provide controls for personalized advertising, depending on your location.

The current app does not use Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. These third-party services are also governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Hifadhi, usalama na chaguo zako

Taarifa za ndani hubaki kwenye kifaa hadi uzifute kwenye programu, ufute data ya programu kwenye mipangilio ya kifaa, au uondoe programu. Kuondoa programu hufuta taarifa za ndani ambazo hazijahifadhiwa kwingine.

Unaweza kuwasha au kuzima vikumbusho vya novena kwenye mipangilio ya programu na kudhibiti ruhusa ya arifa kupitia mipangilio ya kifaa. Unaweza pia kudhibiti matangazo kupitia mipangilio ya Google/kifaa. Kwa ombi la kufuta taarifa zozote ulizotutumia moja kwa moja, wasiliana nasi kwa kutumia maelezo hapa chini.

Tunatumia hifadhi ya ndani ya kifaa na watoa huduma wakubwa wa programu kwa kazi zilizoelezwa. Hakuna njia ya kuhifadhi au kutuma data iliyo salama kwa asilimia 100; tunapunguza taarifa tunazotumia kwa kile kinachohitajika kwa vipengele hivi.

Watoto

Programu ni ya maombi na tafakari za kiroho kwa hadhira ya jumla. Hatuombi watoto watume taarifa binafsi. Mzazi au mlezi akiamini mtoto ametutumia taarifa binafsi, atuwasiliane ili tuweze kushughulikia ombi hilo.

Haki zako

Chini ya sheria zinazotumika za ulinzi wa taarifa binafsi, unaweza kuomba kufahamu au kupata taarifa zako, kusahihisha taarifa zisizo sahihi, kuomba kufutwa au kuwekewa kikomo kwa uchakataji, kupinga uchakataji pale sheria inaporuhusu, kuhamisha taarifa, au kuondoa ridhaa pale uchakataji unapotegemea ridhaa. Tuma ombi kwa mawasiliano hapa chini. Unaweza pia kuwasiliana na Tume ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi Tanzania (PDPC).

Ukurasa huu wa sera

Ukurasa huu huhifadhi chaguo lako la lugha tu kwenye hifadhi ya kivinjari chako (local storage); hautumii cookies za ufuatiliaji au analytics. Huduma ya hosting inaweza kuchakata kumbukumbu za kawaida za maunganisho kwa ajili ya usalama na uwasilishaji wa ukurasa.

Mabadiliko ya sera

Tunaweza kusasisha sera hii programu au huduma zake zinapobadilika. Tarehe ya “Ilisasishwa” itaonyesha toleo la karibuni; tafadhali kagua ukurasa huu mara kwa mara.

Ikiwa una swali kuhusu taarifa zako au sera hii, wasiliana nasi kupitia barua pepe au simu iliyo hapa chini.

3. Storage, security, and your choices

Local information remains on your device until you clear it in the app, clear the app’s data in device settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes local information that has not been stored elsewhere.

You can turn novena reminders on or off in app settings and control notification permission in your device settings. You can also manage advertising through Google/device settings. To ask us to delete information you sent to us directly, contact us using the details below.

We use local device storage and established app-service providers for the functions described here. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; we limit the information used to what these features need.

Children

The app provides prayers and spiritual reflections for a general audience. We do not ask children to submit personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us so we can address the request.

Your rights

Under applicable data-protection law, you may ask whether we hold information about you and request access, correction of inaccurate information, erasure or restriction of processing, object to processing where the law allows, request portability, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Send a request using the contact details below. You may also contact the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

This policy page

This page stores only your language choice in your browser's local storage; it does not use tracking cookies or analytics. The hosting provider may process standard connection logs for security and page delivery.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version; please review this page periodically.

If you have a question about your information or this policy, contact us using the email or phone number below.
